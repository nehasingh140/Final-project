01 — Our Story

Started with one roaster and a lot of stubbornness.

Millhouse began in a garage with a 1kg sample roaster and a simple idea: most coffee sold nearby was old, over-roasted, and forgettable. We started roasting small batches for friends, then a few local cafés, and eventually opened our own space. We still roast in small runs — usually under 15kg at a time — so every bag is close to its roast date.

  • 01 Direct-trade relationships with growers
  • 02 Roasted weekly, never sitting in storage
  • 03 Every batch cupped and logged before release
